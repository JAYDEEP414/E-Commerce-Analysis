# 📊 Flipkart Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of a simulated Flipkart sales dataset containing 50,000 records. The objective is to extract meaningful business insights, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making using Python and data analysis techniques.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall business performance (Revenue & Profit)
* Identify product and category-level trends
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on sales and profitability
* Understand customer behavior and regional performance
* Measure return rates and their effect on revenue
* Generate insights for business growth and optimization

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

The dataset includes detailed transactional data with the following key features:

* Order details (Order ID, Date, Status)
* Product information (Category, Sub-category, Brand)
* Customer details (City, State)
* Financial metrics (Revenue, Profit, Discount)
* Operational metrics (Shipping cost, Delivery time)
* Customer feedback (Ratings, Returns)

Time Period: **2023 – 2025**

## 📈 Key Analysis Performed

### 🔹 Business Performance

* Year-wise Revenue and Profit analysis
* Month-over-Month (MoM) growth calculation
* Identification of peak and low-performing months

### 🔹 Product Insights

* Top-performing products by revenue
* Product-level growth and decline analysis

### 🔹 Category Analysis

* Revenue contribution by category (%)
* Profit margin comparison across categories

### 🔹 Discount Impact

* Relationship between discount percentage and sales
* Profitability impact of discount strategies

### 🔹 Customer Analysis

* Top-performing cities and states
* Repeat customer rate calculation

### 🔹 Seasonal Trends

* Festival vs non-festival sales comparison
* Identification of seasonal spikes

### 🔹 Returns & Loss Analysis

* Return rate percentage
* Revenue loss due to returns

### 🔹 Operational Insights

* Delivery time vs customer rating relationship
* Platform (Mobile/Desktop/App) performance analysis

---

## 📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* Return Rate (%)
* Profit Margin (%)
* Growth Rate (%)

---

## 📌 Key Insights

* Revenue shows a consistent upward trend across years with seasonal fluctuations.
* Certain categories dominate overall sales contribution.
* Discounts increase sales volume but may reduce profit margins.
* Festival periods significantly boost revenue.
* Higher return rates negatively impact overall profitability.
* Delivery time influences customer satisfaction and ratings.
